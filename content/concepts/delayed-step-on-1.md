# Delayed step on 1

*Technique · level 2* — imported from the teaching syllabus and **not yet reviewed**.

Waiting through count 1 and taking the step late, compressing the movement into what is left of the beat. The connection has to stay alive during the wait, or the delay reads as a mistake.
