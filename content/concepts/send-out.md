# Send-out — closed to open

*Pattern · level 1* — imported from the teaching syllabus and **not yet reviewed**.

Opening from closed position to release the follower back down the slot into open position. It is a left-side pass that begins in closed rather than open — the leader opens the frame instead of stepping off the line — which is why the same pattern is also taught as a left-side pass from closed position.
