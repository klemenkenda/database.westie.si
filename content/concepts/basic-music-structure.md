# Basic music structure

*Musicality · level 1* — imported from the teaching syllabus and **not yet reviewed**.

How the songs the dance is done to are built — beats grouped into bars of four, bars into eight- and sixteen-beat phrases, phrases into verses and choruses. Knowing where you are in that structure is what makes hitting a break possible.
