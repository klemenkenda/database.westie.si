# Continuing on the "wrong" foot

*Technique · level 3* — imported from the teaching syllabus and **not yet reviewed**.

Carrying on when you end up on the foot you did not expect. The fix is to adjust the footwork quietly and keep the pattern and the timing intact, not to stop and reset.
