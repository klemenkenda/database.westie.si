# Anchor step variations (slide, knee, cross, drunken sailor)

*Technique · level 3* — imported from the teaching syllabus and **not yet reviewed**.

Ways of dancing the anchor other than a triple in place: a slide, a knee, a crossed anchor, a drunken sailor sway. They change the look while keeping the anchor's job — staying back, holding the stretch and ending the pattern.
