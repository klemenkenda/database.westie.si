# Triple step

*Footwork · level 0 · foundation tier `self`*

Three weight changes in two beats, counted "3 & 4". The unit that every WCS pattern is assembled from — six-count is walk-walk-triple-triple, eight-count adds a third triple. Taught as footwork, but it is really a timing skill.

## Why it is in the foundation

Solo competence. None of it involves a partner, and all of it is a prerequisite for everything that does. This tier is entirely absent from the import, which is the single biggest hole in the graph.
