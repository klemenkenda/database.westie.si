# Rolling-ball momentum

*Connection · level 1* — imported from the teaching syllabus and **not yet reviewed**.

Once the follower is moving, they keep moving in that direction until something changes it — like a rolling ball. The leader's job is to start it, then redirect it, not to carry it the whole way.
